imoe.uk · KUN Galgame file delivery

This domain delivers files, not pages.

imoe.uk is the download domain of the KUN Galgame community — an open-source, non-commercial visual-novel community. Every URL under dl.imoe.uk is a direct, attachment-only download of a community-uploaded game patch archive. There is no other web content here: no forms, no logins, no scripts, no ads, no tracking.

the only kind of URL served here

What it serves

Fan-made translation patches and modification archives for PC visual novels (galgame), uploaded by registered members of our community sites and served verbatim as compressed archives. Files download directly — this domain never asks the visitor to do anything.

Uploading requires an authenticated account on one of the community sites below, so every file is attributable to an accountable user. Reported or flagged files are taken down promptly.

Who we are

KUN Galgame builds free, open-source websites for the Chinese visual-novel community — a forum, a patch-resource site, a wiki and the shared infrastructure behind them. Everything is developed in the open on GitHub.

  • github.com/kungal — UI library, editor, tooling
  • github.com/KunMoe — forum & patch-site source code
  • t.me/kungalgame — community Telegram group

Report a file

If a file distributed through this domain looks harmful or infringing, tell us and we will act on it promptly: open an issue at kungal/kungal-dl or reach the moderators in the Telegram group. Machine-readable contact details are published in security.txt.
